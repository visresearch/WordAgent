# Midnight Galaxy

A dramatic and cosmic theme with deep purples and mystical tones for impactful Word documents.

## Color Palette

- **Deep Purple**: `#2b1e3e` - Rich dark base
- **Cosmic Blue**: `#4a4e8f` - Mystical mid-tone
- **Lavender**: `#a490c2` - Soft accent color
- **Silver**: `#e6e6fa` - Light highlights and text

## Typography

- **Headers**: FreeSans Bold
- **Body Text**: FreeSans

## Best Used For

Entertainment, gaming, nightlife, luxury brand, and creative agency documents.
