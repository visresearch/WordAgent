# Company Newsletter

Create a company-wide digest for employees. Confirm the coverage period and audience when unclear.

## Priorities

- company-wide impact and leadership announcements
- major product, customer, operational, or people milestones
- decisions and upcoming dates that affect many employees
- authoritative links supplied by the user or returned by an available MCP tool

Exclude duplicated announcements, confidential details, and overly granular team activity. Never invent links or imply that a source was reviewed when it was unavailable.

## Structure

Use a clear title and coverage period, followed by 3-6 topic-based sections such as Company Updates, Product and Customers, People, Operations, and Upcoming Dates. Use short bullets, normally one or two sentences each. Around 8-20 bullets is a useful default, but scale to the available facts rather than filling a quota.

Write in an inclusive company voice when appropriate. Put the most consequential items first. Every bullet should answer at least one of: what changed, why it matters, what happens next, or what the reader must do.

End with a short action or contact section only when the source information provides a real action, owner, or channel.
