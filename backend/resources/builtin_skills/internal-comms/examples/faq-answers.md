# Employee FAQs

Turn recurring employee questions into concise, authoritative answers. Select questions with broad relevance or material impact.

## Format

```text
Q: [One clear question]
A: [Direct answer in one to three short paragraphs]
Source or owner: [Authoritative reference, when available]
```

Group related questions under informative headings when the list is long. Lead each answer with the conclusion, then explain essential context, timing, and action.

Use official facts supplied by the user, present in the active document or uploaded files, or returned by an available MCP source. Do not invent policy, approvals, dates, links, or executive positions. Label unknown or changing information and identify the responsible owner when known. If an answer requires legal, HR, security, or executive approval, draft it as pending review rather than presenting it as final.

Keep the tone professional and approachable. Remove questions that merely repeat another answer.
