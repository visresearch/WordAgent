# 3P Updates

Write a short Progress, Plans, and Problems update for leaders and teammates who have limited context. Confirm the team name and reporting period if they are not already known.

## Content

- **Progress**: outcomes completed during the past reporting period. Prioritize shipped work, achieved milestones, measurable results, and decisions.
- **Plans**: the highest-priority outcomes for the next reporting period.
- **Problems**: current blockers, risks, dependencies, or capacity constraints. State the impact and owner or required help when known.

Scale detail to the team's size. Use supplied facts, the current Word document, uploaded files, and only MCP sources that are actually available. Do not infer completion, metrics, or blockers from weak signals.

## Format

Use this structure and keep it readable in 30-60 seconds:

```text
[Team Name] | [Date range]

Progress
- [One to three concise, outcome-focused bullets]

Plans
- [One to three concise, priority-focused bullets]

Problems
- [One to three concise blockers, or "None reported"]
```

Prefer concrete verbs and metrics. Do not use decorative emoji unless the user or existing company style calls for it.
